A collection of 100 jazz pieces that were heard outside and found using music recognition software or through other means.

By: Author(s) of the music
Rating: The rating I gave to the music on the specific listening in which I found and noted it down whilst outdoors
Did I Recognise: If I recognised the music whilst listening
Remark: An additional remark given to each piece of music
Asterisk: If there are special circumstances regarding the rating, remark, by category, recognition category or inclusion into the collection of data in any other way
